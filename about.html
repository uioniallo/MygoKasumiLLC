Who We Are

Mygo Kasumi LLC is a professional business services firm headquartered in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Founded with a vision to empower businesses of all sizes, we combine deep industry expertise with a personalized approach to deliver solutions that truly make a difference.

Our name reflects our commitment to forward momentum — "Mygo" embodies the drive to keep moving forward, while "Kasumi" represents clarity and precision in everything we do.

From startups finding their footing to established enterprises seeking new growth, we tailor our services to meet you exactly where you are and take you where you want to go.
